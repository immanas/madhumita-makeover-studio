Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

How long does bridal makeup last? +

Our bridal makeup is formulated to last 14–18 hours. We use premium waterproof, long-wearing bases and setting sprays designed for Indian weather conditions.

Do you travel to the venue? +

Absolutely. We provide home and venue visits across Kolkata and surrounding areas. Destination weddings across West Bengal are also welcome — travel charges apply.

How early should I book? +

We recommend booking at least 3–6 months in advance for bridal appointments, especially for peak wedding season (October–February). Slots fill quickly!

Is a trial session available? +

Yes — a bridal trial session is included in our Radiance and Luxury packages. We strongly recommend it to finalize your look before the big day.

Do you provide touch-up services? +

Yes. Touch-up kits are provided with our bridal packages. Extended touch-up attendance at the venue can be arranged at an additional charge.

What brands do you use? +

We exclusively use MAC, Huda Beauty, Kryolan, NARS, Bobbi Brown, and Lakmé Pro — ensuring skin-safe, high-performance results every time.
